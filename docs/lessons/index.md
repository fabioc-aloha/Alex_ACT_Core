# Lessons

Durable, reusable lessons captured from real sessions. Each record states one
lesson, the signals that indicate it applies, and what to do about it.

These are context, not instructions. Apply a lesson only when its preconditions
match the situation in front of you, and update or supersede a record rather than
working around it when it turns out to be wrong.
